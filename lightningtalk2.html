Presenter Notes

A few years ago, building an app required learning programming or hiring developers. Today, AI is changing that.

This is RootMap, an early childhood education app I'm building. I'm a business student, but AI coding tools have made it possible for me to develop a functioning product.

Vibe coding won't kill the App Store. It will change who creates software, how personalized software becomes, and what skills make a product valuable.

Now imagine anyone can build an app. What makes one worth using?

# OIM3690 2026 Fall Week 06

## App Ideas

1. **Goal & Habit Tracker** — An app that stores my goals (short-term todos and longer-term life goals) as data, tracks my daily progress against each one, and uses that history to suggest one concrete action to take today that moves me closer to a future goal.
2. **Student Lesson Checklist** — An app that keeps a record for each child in my kindergarten class, tracking which lessons/activities they've completed versus not yet experienced, so I can see each child's progress and know what to plan for them next.
3. **Lesson Prep Planner** — An app that stores lesson data (materials needed, prep time required, and age/duration info) and calculates how long each lesson takes to prepare and when each material needs to be ready, so I know exactly what to prep and when to hand it to the children.
